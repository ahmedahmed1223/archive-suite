Outcome

Manage accounts, monitor system health, and carry out sensitive operations with a reviewable audit trail.

Before you begin

Review system status and errors, then identify the users or services affected by the operation.

Procedure

  1. Open User management and grant the least-privileged role that fits the person’s responsibility.
  2. Review System status before an operational change.
  3. Run the operation once, then review its result and audit entry.

Verify

Confirm the expected outcome and verify that the actor and operation status were recorded.

Safety

Do not broaden permissions to work around an operational issue, and do not repeat a sensitive action before checking the first result.
