Outcome

Check system health, manage backups, and carry out recovery or support work with clear evidence.

Before you begin

Identify the affected service or data and review the latest backup and error history before acting.

Procedure

  1. Open System status and identify the affected component and the time the issue began.
  2. Review Backups and select a verified copy if recovery is required.
  3. Run the operation once, then review status, errors, and the audit entry.

Verify

Confirm that the expected service or data is available and that the actor and operation status were recorded.

Safety

Do not restore a copy before previewing its impact, and do not repeat a sensitive action before checking the first result.
