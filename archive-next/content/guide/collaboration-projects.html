Outcome

Organize related material in a collection or project and coordinate work without losing the context of each change.

Before you begin

Define the collection or project goal, its participants, and the records included in the work.

Procedure

  1. Open Projects and create a project named for the result you need.
  2. Add records and tasks, then assign an owner and due date to each step.
  3. Use comments and collaboration tools to record decisions and update the work status.

Verify

Confirm that the right participants can see the material and tasks and that the latest update and its author are recorded.

Safety

Do not add a person or item to a project before reviewing access scope and rights.
