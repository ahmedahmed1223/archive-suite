Outcome

Add an accurate, searchable item with a clear description and reviewable rights information.

Before you begin

Confirm that the file is the correct version and gather its title, source, and rights status.

Procedure

  1. Open Uploads and choose the file, or create a record without a file when appropriate.
  2. Write a clear title and a description that will help the team find the material.
  3. Set the type, source, and rights, then submit the item and monitor processing.

Verify

Open the record after upload and check the attachment, preview, and descriptive metadata.

Safety

Do not upload an untrusted file or material the organization is not entitled to preserve.
