Outcome

Browse files, open previews, and download an allowed copy of the correct material.

Before you begin

Know the file name or its related record and confirm that your role permits access to the material.

Procedure

  1. Open Files and locate the required folder or item.
  2. Use the preview to confirm the content before downloading it.
  3. Download only when the rights status and access restrictions permit it.

Verify

Compare the file name, size, and type with the record, then open the downloaded copy to confirm its integrity.

Safety

Do not move downloaded files to an unapproved location, and do not identify content by file name alone.
