Outcome

Sign in to the workspace, understand the main navigation, and reach the first record relevant to your task.

Before you begin

Make sure you have a valid account and know whether your role is Viewer, Editor, or Administrator.

Procedure

  1. Sign in, then check your name and role in the application header.
  2. Use the main navigation to open Search, Files, or Help.
  3. Open one record and review its description, attachments, and rights information.

Verify

Confirm that the visible navigation and guide chapters fit your role and that you can open a record without a permission error.
