Outcome

Track audio or video processing, review the output, and keep it attached to the correct record.

Before you begin

Confirm that the file plays and that the record accurately identifies the material, source, and language.

Whisper processor

Administrators can choose the processor for new transcription jobs in Settings. CPU is the default. Choose GPU only when the Whisper environment is prepared for CUDA.

Procedure

  1. Open Media jobs and locate the job for the record.
  2. Monitor its status, then open the preview or generated transcript when processing completes.
  3. Review start and end points and audio or image quality, then save any permitted correction.

Verify

Play at least two positions in the material and confirm that the output matches both the file and record.

Safety

Do not approve automated output without review, and never replace the original source with a processed copy.
