Outcome

Understand an item’s rights status and create a share that stays within the permitted duration and scope.

Before you begin

Review the rights holder, permitted use, and any time or territory restrictions recorded for the item.

Procedure

  1. Open Rights and locate the item or record.
  2. Review the restrictions, then open Shares if the intended use is allowed.
  3. Set the link duration and access scope, create the share, and send it only to the intended recipient.

Verify

Open the shares list, confirm the item, duration, and status, then test the link with an appropriate account.

Safety

Revoke the link as soon as it is no longer needed, and never use sharing to bypass a rights or permission restriction.
