Outcome

Review system settings, test storage, database, and integration connections, and personalize how the workspace looks and behaves for your own account.

Before you begin

Prepare approved connection details and identify the environment being checked and who may change it.

Preset profiles

A preset applies a ready-made bundle of home page, navigation order, hidden modules, and archive view in one action: Archivist (the archive as home, a detailed table view, capture and organization first in navigation), Reviewer (the daily list as home, collaboration and review first, heavy import tools hidden), Media editor (media jobs as home, a visual grid view, system administration tools hidden), or Simple view (the fewest navigation items and archive columns, for quick, uncluttered use). Applying a preset overwrites the matching personal settings below; you can still adjust anything afterward.

Navigation and view customization

Reorder the navigation's sections with the up and down controls, and hide modules you do not use — a mandatory item (like Settings) cannot be hidden, and a module a capability has switched off is hidden automatically and cannot be forced visible from here. For the archive table, choose which columns show beyond the always-visible title, and set a saved search as the default filter that opens with the archive.

Home activity summary

The home page's activity panel reports how many records were added in the last seven days, what share of records have a complete description, and how records are distributed across types — every figure comes from a live search, so a metric shows as unavailable rather than a guess when the server has nothing to report.

Administration

Administrators see an additional section that turns organization-wide capabilities — system control, backups, trash, the legacy-database bridge, semantic search, media processing, OCR, and MCP — on or off, each with its current status and, when it is locked, the reason and source of the lock. A capability that needs deployment configuration cannot be turned on from here alone. Task escalation timing and target durations live under Project tasks, and per-operation approval requirements are enforced by the platform rather than edited from this section.

Procedure

  1. Open Settings and review identity, security, storage, and integrations.
  2. Run the required connection test and read its message, time, and environment before taking action.
  3. Change only the permitted setting, then test again and review System status.
  4. Apply a preset for a quick starting point, or adjust navigation, view, and notification settings individually.
  5. Administrators: review capability status under Administration.

Verify

Confirm that the test passes, that the intended service works from inside the application, and that the workspace now opens to the home page, navigation, and columns you configured.

Safety

Do not place connection secrets in notes or screenshots, and do not test writes against production data without approval.
