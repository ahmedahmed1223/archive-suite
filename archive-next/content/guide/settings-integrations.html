Outcome

Review system settings and test storage, database, and integration connections from one place.

Before you begin

Prepare approved connection details and identify the environment being checked and who may change it.

Presets and personal layout

Every user has their own locale, theme, navigation order, and archive view under Settings — these apply to that account only. A preset (Archivist, Reviewer, Media editor, or Simple) applies a ready-made bundle of these choices in one action. Administrators additionally see an Administration section that turns organization-wide capabilities on or off; a capability that needs deployment configuration cannot be turned on from Settings alone.

Procedure

  1. Open Settings and review identity, security, storage, and integrations.
  2. Run the required connection test and read its message, time, and environment before taking action.
  3. Change only the permitted setting, then test again and review System status.
  4. Administrators: review capability status and task escalation and approval policy under Administration.

Verify

Confirm that the test passes and that the intended service works from inside the application.

Safety

Do not place connection secrets in notes or screenshots, and do not test writes against production data without approval.
