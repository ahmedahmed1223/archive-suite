Outcome

Create appropriate accounts and grant each person the least-privileged role that meets their responsibilities.

Before you begin

Confirm the person’s identity, responsibility, and the authority that approved their access.

Procedure

  1. Open Users and roles and review existing accounts to avoid duplicates.
  2. Create the invitation with the correct email and choose Viewer, Editor, or Administrator according to need.
  3. Review the account after invitation acceptance and change the role when responsibilities change.

Verify

Confirm that the person can complete their task and cannot see functions beyond their role.

Safety

Do not grant Administrator access for day-to-day convenience, and remove access when it is no longer needed.
