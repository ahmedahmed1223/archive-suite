Outcome

Understand the key improvements in version 1.1 and choose the best place to begin.

Before you begin

Sign in and confirm your account role because some capabilities and chapters are available only to Editors or Administrators.

Included in version 1.1

  • Help and a user guide in Arabic and English that follow the interface language.
  • Fully supported native packages for Windows and Linux, alongside the Docker path.
  • Choose the interface language in Settings, with your choice saved for your next visit.

Procedure

  1. Select the interface language in Settings.
  2. Open the chapter for your task and follow its verification steps.
  3. Review System status if an operation does not complete as expected.

Verify

Confirm that the interface and guide use the same language and that your selected path suits your environment and account permissions.
