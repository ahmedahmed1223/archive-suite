Outcome

Understand the capabilities available in the current release and choose the best place to begin.

Included in this release

  • Arabic and English interfaces, selected in Settings and saved to the account.
  • Fully supported native packages for Windows and Linux, alongside the Docker path.
  • Search, records, files, rights, sharing, collaboration, and administration in one interface.

Procedure

  1. Select the interface language in Settings.
  2. Open the chapter for your task and follow its verification steps.
  3. Review System status if an operation does not complete as expected.

Verify

Confirm that the interface and guide use the same language and that the visible chapters match your account permissions.
