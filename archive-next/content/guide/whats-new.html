Outcome

Understand the current capabilities and choose the best place to begin your task.

Before you begin

Sign in and confirm your account role because some capabilities and chapters are available only to Editors or Administrators.

Current capabilities

  • Native packages are fully supported for local operation and supported distributions, while preserving the same operational path across supported environments.
  • Choose the interface language in Settings to use operational tools and messages in Arabic or English.
  • Stronger security protections: real content-security enforcement, request rate limiting, and cross-origin controls are now active, not just displayed as settings.
  • More reliable encrypted backups: encrypted backups now stream instead of loading the whole archive into memory, so large archives back up and restore reliably.
  • The legacy-database (ODBC) bridge now works correctly against SQL Server, PostgreSQL, and MySQL, and reports real errors instead of looking empty when something fails.
  • The application now reports its actual connection health to the backend, so operators can tell the difference between "running" and "actually working".

Procedure

  1. Review Settings for any security configuration your deployment relies on (rate limits, allowed origins).
  2. Open the chapter for your task and follow its verification steps.
  3. Review System status if an operation does not complete as expected.

Verify

Confirm that the interface behaves as expected and that any encrypted backups you rely on restore correctly.
